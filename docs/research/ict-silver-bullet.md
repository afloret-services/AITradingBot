# The ICT Silver Bullet Setup: what ICT defines, and what he leaves open

Research for GitHub issue #6. Question: what does ICT's (Michael J. Huddleston's) own material define the Silver Bullet Setup as, and where does it leave room for interpretation? This feeds the grilling session that will fix the bot's exact Setup rules for MNQ.

Vocabulary follows `GLOSSARY.md`: a **Setup** is the written rules, a **Signal** is one time the rules are met, and a **Trade** is the position opened from one Signal.

## 2. What the Setup is (the near-primary core)

- **A time-based model that works in any market.** The Glasp summary calls it "a time-based algorithmic framework that identifies high-probability trade setups across all asset classes, such as indices and forex" ([Glasp, T2](https://glasp.co/youtube/p/2023-ict-mentorship-ict-silver-bullet-time-based-trading-model)).
- **Its core is an FVG traded inside a 60-minute window.** ICT Sharks notes: "The Silver Bullet setups are simply Fair Value Gaps that exist inside of a 60-minute window", and then "it does not mean the FVGs must be formed in this time period! Only trade entry must take place." ([ICT Sharks, T2](https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/2023-ict-mentorship-ict-silver-bullet-time-based-trading-model/358)). **This is the most important point for a bot, and it is the one most third-party guides contradict (see §6).**
- **The Setup is chosen by its draw on liquidity.** The lecture lists the contexts the model trades from: previous daily high/low as the draw on liquidity, previous weekly high/low, previous session high/low, a return to the current or an old New Week Opening Gap (NWOG), expansion away from the current or an old NWOG, a classic ICT Optimal Trade Entry (OTE), and confluence with the 2022 ICT Mentorship model. It stresses that the main job is working out the next most likely draw in price ([ICT Sharks, T2](https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/2023-ict-mentorship-ict-silver-bullet-time-based-trading-model/358); [Glasp, T2](https://glasp.co/youtube/p/2023-ict-mentorship-ict-silver-bullet-time-based-trading-model)).
- **It requires a minimum framework (a minimum room to the target).** Index futures need at least 10 points (40 ticks) and FX at least 15 pips. The framework is "the best-case price delivery that you expect to see, not an exact trade entry-to-exit range". Trades that miss it "are generally low probability and better ignored" ([ICT Sharks, T2](https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/2023-ict-mentorship-ict-silver-bullet-time-based-trading-model/358)). One summary gives the conversion as "10 handles in indices is the same as 20 pips in Forex, 5 handles ... 10 pips" (search excerpt of the ICT Sharks/Coconote notes; [Coconote transcript, T2/T3](https://coconote.app/notes/d45c86e2-52cd-4cac-b958-b18513dd3b11/transcript)).
- **Trades can be held past the hour.** The trade "is many times held in duration longer than the window that the framework and entry is derived from". The worked example: shorting into the FVG and covering at the old low "allows for at least 5 handles" ([ICT Sharks, T2](https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/2023-ict-mentorship-ict-silver-bullet-time-based-trading-model/358)). A transcript excerpt adds that reaching 10 handles is not required ("We don't require that", said of a trade that reached five) ([Coconote, T2/T3](https://coconote.app/notes/d45c86e2-52cd-4cac-b958-b18513dd3b11/transcript)).

- The times are **New York local**, so they move with US daylight saving. Several T3 sources get the GMT/UTC conversions wrong ([LuxAlgo concept page](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/silver-bullet/); [ultimamarkets](https://www.ultimamarkets.com/academy/ict-silver-bullet-times-to-trade/)). The bot should work in `America/New_York`.
- **Killzones are a separate, wider concept.** T3 consensus puts the London Open killzone at about 02:00–05:00 NY and the New York killzone at about 07:00–10:00 NY (some say 07:00–09:00, others stretch it to 11:00). Each Silver Bullet hour sits inside or next to one of them ([chartinglens](https://chartinglens.com/blog/ict-kill-zones-guide); [ictkillzone.com](https://www.ictkillzone.com/ict-kill-zones); [audacity.capital](https://audacity.capital/trading-guides/ict-kill-zones/)). I could not reach an ICT-primary statement of the killzone times in this session. The 10:00–11:00 AM SB actually starts **after** the usual NY killzone ends.
- **The SB hour ties into ICT's other time-based ideas.** ICT's own post links the 10:00 SB to the Opening Range Gap: "When Indices gap lower at 9:30 am ET the first concern is the 70% odds Opening Range Gap C.E. is booked by 10:00 am ET. If it fails to fill that number, use 10:00 am ET Silver Bullet to ORG C.E. long." ([X, T1](https://x.com/I_Am_The_ICT/status/1897984651186114746)). In other words, ICT treats the SB as one tool inside a wider daily narrative, not as a standalone mechanical rule.
- **Variants that are T3 only:** "Type 1: SMT / Type 2: Stop Hunt" ([Liquidity Sniper](https://x.com/Liq_Sniper/status/1716480689766547932), credited to ICT, unverified); "the ICT 2022 Model can be applied to Silver Bullet" (a clip of ICT, [A+ trades](https://x.com/theAplustrades/status/1662282445952098305)), which agrees with the lecture's "confluence with the 2022 model" item.

## 4. The ICT ideas the Setup builds on

### Fair value gap (FVG)
- ICT teaches it in the Mentorship Core Content, Month 04, "ICT Fair Value Gaps FVG" ([archive.org listing](https://archive.org/details/23333333333333333331ac)). I could not reach a primary transcript.
- Near-universal T3 reading: a **three-candle pattern**. A bullish FVG is the gap between candle 1's **high** and candle 3's **low** when they do not overlap. A bearish FVG is the gap between candle 1's **low** and candle 3's **high**. Candle 2 is the displacement candle ([fxopen](https://fxopen.com/blog/en/what-is-the-ict-silver-bullet-strategy-and-how-does-it-work/); [Studocu note on Month 04, T3](https://www.studocu.com/in/document/netaji-vidyapith-railway-higher-secondary-school/computer-science-sl/ict-fair-value-gap-fvg-ict-mentorship-core-co/99980817)). The gap is measured wick to wick, not body to body.
- **Consequent encroachment (C.E.)** is the 50% midpoint of the gap. ICT uses "C.E." in his own post above ([X, T1](https://x.com/I_Am_The_ICT/status/1897984651186114746)), and T3 notes define it as the 50% level of any gap ([fxnx](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/ict-consequent-encroachment-precision-fvg-entry); [innercircletrader.net, T3](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-2024/ict-2024-mentorship-lecture-4/)).
- **"First presented FVG"** is a separate ICT idea (from the 2024 mentorship): the first FVG after a session open matters most. It overlaps with the SB in trader discussion but is not the same rule ([relentlessADD on X, T3](https://x.com/relentlessADD/status/1827077762542121346); [AlterEgoTrader, T3](https://x.com/AlterEgoTrader/status/1863919363398492163)).
- **Which timeframe to find FVGs on:** the 2022 mentorship notes say ICT looks at 1-, 2-, 3- and 5-minute charts together for index imbalances ([ICT Sharks 2022 Ep. 2 notes, T2](https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-2-elements-to-a-trade-setup/146)).

### Liquidity sweep / draw on liquidity
- The **draw on liquidity** is the level price is expected to move to, such as old highs/lows, where buy stops rest above and sell stops below ([Coconote, T2/T3](https://coconote.app/notes/d45c86e2-52cd-4cac-b958-b18513dd3b11/transcript)). The SB lecture lists the eligible draws (§2).
- The lecture also allows an "inefficiency" (an opposing FVG) as the draw. A transcript excerpt says a bearish trader may aim for "a discount buy-side imbalance, sell-side inefficiency, or some measure of fair value gap" below price ([Coconote](https://coconote.app/notes/d45c86e2-52cd-4cac-b958-b18513dd3b11/transcript)).
- A **sweep** (taking an old high/low) *before* the displacement comes from the 2022 model: "look for buy stops to be run, then a break in market structure ... short in the FVG, profit target at the most recent low nearby" ([TanjaTrades 2022 notes, T3](https://www.scribd.com/document/666181194/ICT-2022-Mentorship-Notes-TanjaTrades); [2022 Ep. 3 notes](https://www.scribd.com/document/741686453/ICT-Mentorship-2022-Episode-3-Notes)). The SB lecture notes, as reached, **do not list a sweep as a hard requirement**. Most T3 guides add one anyway (§6).

### Market structure shift (MSS)
- In the 2022 Mentorship Episode 3 ("Internal Range Liquidity & Market Structure Shifts", [ICT Sharks, T2](https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-3-internal-range-liquidity-market-structure-shifts/147); video ID `nQfHZ2DEJ8c` per [videohighlight](https://videohighlight.com/v/nQfHZ2DEJ8c)), an MSS is a **short-term** swing high/low broken by the move away from a sweep. Shifts are intraday events, as opposed to "breaks" that start lasting trends ([2022 Ep. 3 notes, T3](https://www.scribd.com/document/741686453/ICT-Mentorship-2022-Episode-3-Notes)).
- A later definition, attributed to a 2024 lecture: "the initial change in the delivery of price which indicates a short-term change in trend" ([innercircletrader.net, T3](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/)).
- The SB lecture notes, as reached, do **not** list an MSS as a requirement either.

### Displacement
- T3 summaries of the 2022 notes describe sharp, fast moves with strong momentum that break key levels ([Studocu 2022 notes, T3](https://www.studocu.com/row/document/the-state-university-of-zanzibar/research-methods/ict-2022-mentorship-notes-episode-insights-on-market-structure-and-trading/131146787)). The FVG is the trace displacement leaves behind, since candle 2 is the displacement candle. ICT gives **no numeric threshold** in any source I reached.

## 6. Where sources disagree

- **Where the FVG must form.** T2 notes of the lecture say the FVG may form before the window and only the entry must be inside it ([ICT Sharks](https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/2023-ict-mentorship-ict-silver-bullet-time-based-trading-model/358)). T3 guides say the FVG must form inside the window, and that "an identical FVG formed outside the killzone does not constitute a valid Silver Bullet" ([search summary of fxnx/quantum-algo](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/ict-silver-bullet-strategy-complete-guide/)).
- **Is a sweep + MSS required?** T3 mostly says yes ("no displacement, no MSS, no trade"), but argues over whether the sweep can come just before the window ([search summary of aquafutures/forexbee/liquidityscan](https://liquidityscan.io/blog/silver-bullet-strategy-complete-checklist-examples)). The T2 lecture notes frame the model around the draw on liquidity and the FVG, and treat the 2022 model (sweep → MSS → FVG) as *one* of several contexts.
- **Minimum framework for NQ:** 10 points (T2) against 20 points (T3, [Course Hero](https://www.coursehero.com/file/219492502/Understanding-ICT-SB-pdf/)).
- **Number of windows:** 3 (T2) against 4–5 (T3 claims of 07:00–08:30 and 15:00–16:00).
- **Stops:** four different T3 rules, and none from ICT that I could reach.

## 7. Ambiguities the bot must pin down

1. **Which windows are active.** Readings: AM only (10–11); AM + PM; all three (3–4, 10–11, 14–15); plus the unverified 07:00–08:30 / 15:00–16:00. For MNQ, the 03:00 window is in the overnight (Globex) session.
2. **Window boundaries and the time zone.** Whether 10:00:00 is inclusive and 11:00:00 exclusive; whether "inside the window" is judged by bar *open* time or bar *close* time; always in `America/New_York`, with DST handled.
3. **What has to happen inside the window.** (a) only the entry fill (T2); (b) the FVG must form inside it; (c) sweep, MSS and FVG must all happen inside it; (d) sweep before the window is allowed, FVG and entry inside it.
4. **Unfilled order at window end.** Cancel a resting limit at 11:00, or let it fill later?
5. **FVG definition.** Wick-to-wick three-candle gap (standard). Open questions: a minimum gap size in ticks; whether candle 2 must close beyond something; whether candle 2's body size counts as "displacement".
6. **Which FVG.** The first FVG in the window; the first FVG in the bias direction; the first after a sweep/MSS; any FVG; the FVG nearest the draw. And whether a second Signal is allowed after the first Trade.
7. **FVG timeframe.** 1m, 3m, or 5m bars (or several), and whether to find the gap on one timeframe and enter on another.
8. **Entry price.** The near edge of the FVG (first touch); C.E. (50%); the far edge; a market order on the close of candle 3. Also whether the Signal needs price to come back into the gap at all.
9. **Bias / direction.** (a) the draw on liquidity sets the direction (T2); (b) a fixed higher-timeframe rule (daily/H4/1h structure, premium/discount); (c) none, so trade whichever side the in-window displacement takes (both sides). Each needs its own measurable definition.
10. **Draw on liquidity: which levels qualify, and which one is chosen.** Candidates from the lecture: previous day H/L, previous week H/L, previous session H/L (which sessions: Asia, London, NY AM?), NWOG, an opposing FVG/inefficiency. A rule is needed when several qualify (the nearest that meets the minimum framework?).
11. **Is a liquidity sweep required?** And if so: which levels count as swept (session highs/lows? the 1m swing points?), whether a wick through is enough, and how far back to look.
12. **Is an MSS required, and how is it defined?** Which swing counts as "short-term" (a fractal of N bars on which timeframe); a body close beyond it versus a wick through it.
13. **Displacement threshold.** None given. Possible readings: candle 2's range ≥ k × ATR; body/range ratio; consecutive large-bodied candles; or "an FVG was left behind" counting as displacement on its own.
14. **Minimum framework for MNQ.** 10 points (T2) or 20 points (T3), and measured from where (entry? the FVG? the window open?) to the draw. Skip the Signal if it is not met?
15. **Stop placement.** Beyond candle 1 of the FVG; beyond the candle that created the FVG; beyond the displacement swing; plus a tick buffer; a maximum stop in points. ICT gives nothing I could verify.
16. **Target and management.** The full draw on liquidity; a fixed R multiple (2R/3R); a fixed 5/10 handles; partials (e.g. a 5-handle base hit, then the rest to the draw), which would need more than one take-profit in the Bracket order. Whether to move the stop to break-even.
17. **Time stop.** Flat at window end (11:00), at a session-wide cut-off, or held until stop/target (T2 says trades are often held past the hour).
18. **Daily limits.** Number of Signals/Trades per window and per day; whether a loss stops further windows that day.
19. **News and day filters.** The ICT posts reached mention an "NFP Protocol" ([X, T1](https://x.com/I_Am_The_ICT/status/1686752904953081856)), which suggests news-day rules exist. The skip rules are undefined (FOMC, CPI, NFP, half-days).
20. **Interaction with other ICT models.** Whether the Opening Range Gap condition in ICT's own post (gap at 09:30, C.E. not filled by 10:00 → take the SB to ORG C.E.) is part of this Setup or a separate one.
