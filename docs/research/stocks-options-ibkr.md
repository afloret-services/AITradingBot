## Answer in short

1. **Stocks fit #42's Bracket-order design with whole shares.** `ib_async.bracketOrder` is contract-agnostic, so one Bracket order per Take profit carries over to `Stock(...)` on `SMART`. Odd lots (under 100 shares) are ordinary US stock orders. Fractional shares are **not** available through the TWS API for stocks, so sizing floors to whole shares.
2. **Stocks need their own Trading day and an outside-RTH rule.** Regular hours are 09:30–16:00 New York. Pre-market (04:00–09:30) and after-hours (16:00–20:00) accept only limit-type orders flagged `outsideRth`; market and plain stop orders work only in regular hours. `bracketOrder`'s stop leg is a plain `STP`, so a Setup that trades or holds outside 09:30–16:00 needs a custom bracket with a stop-limit leg. IBKR's Overnight session (20:00–03:50, exchange `OVERNIGHT`) is limit-only.
3. **Shorting is possible but gated.** It needs a margin account, IBKR's automatic locate (the "shortable" tick shows availability), a borrow fee on positions held past settlement, and Reg SHO Rule 201 (after a 10% drop, shorts only above the national best bid for the rest of that day and the next).
4. **The $25,000 pattern-day-trader rule is being retired.** The SEC approved FINRA's replacement of Rule 4210's day-trading provisions with an intraday margin standard on 2026-04-14, effective 2026-06-04, with firms allowed to phase it in until about October 2027. IBKR's pages still say an account "may still be subject to existing PDT rules during FINRA's transition period". IBKR simulates the PDT rule on the Paper account. Whether it still does today was not found.
5. **Options: keep the exits in the engine, with a premium-based Stop loss as backstop.** A Setup reading the underlying's chart can exit an option three ways: (a) IBKR conditional orders with a `PriceCondition` on the underlying's `conId`; (b) a bracket on the option priced in option premium; (c) bar-close exit intents from the engine. (c) matches how the backtest decides and needs nothing unverified. (a) works on single orders per IBKR's own option example, but whether conditions work on bracket children and OCA groups was not found. Option stops trigger on "double bid/ask" by default.
6. **Contract selection is data-driven.** `reqSecDefOptParams` returns expirations, strikes, multiplier and trading class per exchange. ATM and N-strikes-OTM need only the underlying's price. Choosing by delta needs option greeks, so it needs OPRA data (live or delayed). 0DTE exists only where the underlying lists that day's expiry, so read it from the chain rather than assume it.
7. **Long options are paid in full and should never be held into expiry.** Options with nine months or less to expiry have no loan value. The OCC exercises anything $0.01 or more in the money at expiry, which turns 1 contract into 100 shares. IBKR may liquidate or lapse positions the account cannot carry. The bot should close options before the close on expiry day.
8. **Market data:** US stocks stream free in real time from Cboe One and IEX (not the NBBO). The consolidated NBBO plus OPRA costs the US Securities Snapshot and Futures Value Bundle (USD 10/month, waived at USD 30 commissions) plus the US Equity and Options Add-On Streaming Bundle (USD 4.50/month). Delayed (15-minute) data is free. Subscriptions are per username, and a live username can share them with its Paper account, but not use them on both at once.

## 1. Stocks: contracts, bracket orders and several Take profits

- **Contract:** `Stock(symbol, 'SMART', 'USD')`, qualified with `qualifyContracts`. `ContractDetails` carries `minTick`, `minSize`, `sizeIncrement`, `stockType`, `timeZoneId`, `tradingHours` and `liquidHours` **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `contract.py:555-580`. Set `primaryExchange` for symbols listed on more than one venue.
- **Bracket orders carry over.** `IB.bracketOrder(action, quantity, limitPrice, takeProfitPrice, stopLossPrice, **kwargs)` builds a `LimitOrder` parent (`transmit=False`), a `LimitOrder` take profit and a `StopOrder` stop, both with `parentId=parent.orderId`, and only the stop has `transmit=True`. Every `**kwargs` goes to all three legs **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `ib.py:694-750`. Nothing in it is specific to futures. #42's rule of one Bracket order per Take profit, and #29's findings on several Setups sharing one net position ([`ibkr-multi-setup-orders.md`](https://github.com/afloret-services/AITradingBot/blob/research/ibkr-multi-setup-orders/docs/research/ibkr-multi-setup-orders.md)), apply to stocks unchanged **[inference]**.
- **Several Take profits:** with whole shares, a Trade of N shares splits into Take profits of whole shares. A Trade too small to split (for example 2 shares into three Take profits) needs the same "merge or skip" rule #42 has for contracts **[inference]**.
- **Odd lots** (fewer than 100 shares) are ordinary orders on US stocks. No IBKR source found treats them differently for brackets or stops **[not found; inference]**. Unlike IDEALPRO forex, no source found adds a price penalty for odd lots.
- **Fractional shares through the API: no.** IBKR's FAQ says fractional trading "is supported via FIX/CTCI but not via API at this time", and IBKR's API notes say the APIs do not support fractional or cash-quantity trading apart from crypto and forex **[IBKR via extract]** [Fractional Shares](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/articles/83261303), [Order types notes and limitations](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/general/order-types/notes-limitations/introduction). The Web API rounds a stock `cashQty` down to whole shares and rejects an order worth less than one share **[IBKR via extract]** [Cash Quantity Orders in the Web API](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/web-api/v1/endpoints/orders/cash-quantity-orders-in-the-web-api). `Order.cashQty` exists in `ib_async` (`order.py:138`), but do not use it for stocks. **Bot rule [inference]:** size = floor(1R budget ÷ (stop distance × 1 share)), and a size of 0 means "stop too wide".
- **Stop triggering on stocks:** IBKR's default trigger method for non-option products is "Last" (US options use double bid/ask) **[IBKR via extract]** [Trigger Methods](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/orders/trigger-methods), [default trigger method FAQ](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/default-trigger-method-stop-order-50654253). It applies only to stops IBKR simulates. Native exchange stops ignore it. The simulated broker can therefore trigger stock stops on trade prices, as for MNQ **[inference]**.

## 2. Stocks: regular and extended hours

| Session (New York) | Where | Order types |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-market 04:00–09:30 | `SMART` with `outsideRth=True` | Limit and stop-limit only |
| Regular 09:30–16:00 | `SMART` | All, including market and stop |
| After-hours 16:00–20:00 | `SMART` with `outsideRth=True` | Limit and stop-limit only |
| Overnight 20:00–03:50, Sunday–Friday | `exchange='OVERNIGHT'`, `primaryExchange` = listing venue | Limit only (one IBKR article also lists Adaptive) |

- **Outside RTH:** "To have the order active in all sessions including the Premarket hours, Regular Trading Hours and the Aftermarket hours, you must use a Limit or Stop Limit type order and add the attribute 'Allow Outside RTH'." Market and stop orders are active only 09:30–16:00 **[IBKR via extract]** [How can I trade stocks during the pre-market or post-market?](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/trade-stocks-during-pre-market-1163236986), [Outside RTH](https://www1.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/orders/outside-rth.php). In the API this is `Order.outsideRth` **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `order.py:45`.
- **Consequence for brackets [inference]:** `bracketOrder`'s stop is a `StopOrder` (`STP`). Even with `outsideRth=True` passed through `**kwargs`, a plain stop will not trigger in pre- or post-market. A stock Setup whose Trading window or Closing time reaches outside 09:30–16:00 needs a hand-built bracket whose Stop loss is a `StopLimitOrder` (`order.py:217`) with a limit offset. A stop-limit can fail to fill on a gap through it, so the backtest must model that.
- **Overnight:** 20:00–03:50 New York, first session Sunday 20:00, last ending Friday 03:50. Trades between 20:00 and midnight carry the next trading day's date. API orders route with `contract.exchange = "OVERNIGHT"` and the listing venue in `primaryExchange`, and market data must use the same routing **[IBKR via extract]** [Overnight Trading](https://www1.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/us-overnight-trading.php), [API overnight trading](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/campus/ibkr-quant-news/api-overnight-trading/), [What is the overnight trading session?](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/overnight-trading-session-455082053). Sources disagree on whether only Day Limit or also Adaptive orders are allowed, and on whether the session needs enabling in Client Portal. Leave overnight out of the first stock version **[inference]**.
- **Trading day [inference]:** the glossary's Trading day is a CME trade date (18:00–17:00). Stocks need their own: one exchange date, regular hours 09:30–16:00, with the NYSE holiday and early-close calendar (13:00 closes). Market sessions and Closing time read New York clock times, so they carry over. Bars built from `useRTH=True` history exclude pre- and post-market.

## 3. Stocks: shorting

- **Locate:** a short sale needs a margin account, and IBKR performs the Reg SHO locate itself. Availability shows in IBKR's Short Stock Availability tool (Client Portal, Support) and in the API **[IBKR via extract]** [Short Sale Cost](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/pricing/short-sale-cost.php).
- **Shortable ticks in the API:** request generic tick `236` with `reqMktData`. It fills `Ticker.shortable` (tick 46) and `Ticker.shortableShares` (tick 89) **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `ib.py:1397`, `wrapper.py:137,144`, `ticker.py:102-103`. Since TWS 974, tick 236 returns the exact number of shares available as well as the level **[IBKR via extract]** [tick types](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/tick_types.html). The level thresholds (commonly quoted as > 2.5 = at least 1,000 shares available, > 1.5 = hard to borrow, located on request, otherwise not available) were **not confirmed**: the extract was cut off. **Bot rule [inference]:** before a short entry, require `shortableShares` ≥ the order size, or skip the Signal and log why.
- **Borrow fees:** the daily cost of a short is the borrow fee less the interest on the short-sale proceeds, and rebates can go negative. "Borrow fee rates and short sale proceeds are calculated based on settled stock positions." The rate charged is the one on the settlement date. Rates are indicative and change intraday. A general-collateral stock can become hard to borrow while held. USD collateral is 102% of the value, rounded up to the dollar **[IBKR via extract]** [Short Sale Cost](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/pricing/short-sale-cost.php). **Consequence [inference]:** a short opened and closed on the same day never settles, so it should pay no borrow fee. A short held overnight pays the fee from settlement (T+1). The fill model needs a borrow-fee line only for multi-day shorts.
- **Recall and buy-in:** a lender can recall borrowed shares and IBKR can close the short. No IBKR page on its notice period was reached **[not found]**.
- **Uptick rule (Reg SHO Rule 201):** when a stock falls 10% or more from the prior close, the listing market triggers the circuit breaker. Short sales may then be executed or displayed only above the current national best bid, "for the remainder of the day and the following day". A further 10% drop on a restricted day re-triggers it. It can trigger only during regular hours, but once triggered it applies whenever a national best bid is disseminated, including extended hours. Sells marked "short exempt" are the exception **[SEC via extract]** [Rule 201 FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/divisions/marketreg/rule201faq.htm), [SEC press release 2010-26](https://www.sec.gov/news/press/2010/2010-26.htm). **Consequences [inference]:** under Rule 201, a short entry by sell stop or sell market cannot fill at or below the bid, so short Setups on falling stocks will miss fills. The backtest needs each day's restricted list, or must refuse short entries on a stock down 10% from its prior close, and on the next day.

## 4. Stocks: the pattern-day-trader rule

- **The old rule:** under FINRA Rule 4210, a margin account with four or more day trades in five business days is a pattern day trader and must keep USD 25,000 of equity. IBKR's FAQ: "if your margin account is under USD 25,000 then you are limited to 3 'day trades' in a rolling 5 business day period", and the third flags it "Potential Pattern Day Trader". A cash account is not subject to the PDT rule **[IBKR via extract]** [How can I determine the number of available Day Trades?](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/determine-number-available-day-trades-28217126), [Why is my account classified as a pattern day trader account?](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/1163236687). TWS shows "Day Trades Left (T, T+1, ..., T+4)" in the Account window's Available for Trading section.
- **The rule has been replaced.** On 2026-04-14 the SEC approved SR-FINRA-2025-017, replacing Rule 4210's day-trading provisions with an intraday margin standard and removing the "pattern day trader" definition and its USD 25,000 equity requirement. FINRA announced it in Regulatory Notice 26-10 (2026-04-20). It took effect on 2026-06-04, and several sources say firms have about 18 months, to around October 2027, to implement it **[FINRA via extract]** [FINRA weekly update 2026-04-15](https://www.finra.org/compliance-tools/weekly-archive/04152026); [SEC Release 34-105226](https://www.sec.gov/rule-release/34-105226) (title only); WilmerHale and King & Spalding alerts **[secondary]**. The USD 2,000 minimum equity to trade on margin (Rule 4210(b)(4)) is unchanged.
- **IBKR's status:** a secondary source quotes an IBKR margin page: "Your account may still be subject to existing PDT rules during FINRA's transition period, which ends in October 2027" **[secondary]** [scalpeltrade.com](https://scalpeltrade.com/blog/pdt-rule-interactive-brokers). What IBKR's new intraday margin check does to an account that day-trades with too little equity was **not found**.
- **On the Paper account:** IBKR "simulates the PDT rule in the paper trading account" when the account would be in scope. The Paper account can be reset in Client Portal to a balance above USD 25,000, which takes it out of scope **[IBKR via extract]** [Does the PDT rule apply to the paper trading account?](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/pattern-day-trader-pdt-rule-37132131). Whether IBKR still simulates the old rule since 2026-06-04 was **not found**.
- **Bot rule [inference]:** keep the Paper account's balance at or above USD 25,000 so the question does not arise in paper trading. For a live account under USD 25,000, read the "Day Trades Left" value from account values (`accountValues` / `accountSummary`; the tag name was not confirmed), and stand down from new stock or option entries when it reaches 0, until IBKR confirms the new standard applies. Futures and forex are not covered by the rule.

## 5. Options: exits on the option from the underlying's chart

A Setup reads the underlying's bars (for example SPY's) and buys a call or put. The Stop loss and Take profits are prices on the underlying. Three ways to carry them to the option:

| Way | What it is | Strength | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Conditional orders | Exit orders on the option, each with a `PriceCondition` on the underlying's `conId`, held until the condition is met | Exits at IBKR even if the bot is down | Combination with brackets and OCA not confirmed; trigger is a tick, not a bar close |
| B. Premium bracket | An ordinary bracket on the option, Stop loss and Take profits priced in option premium (mapped from the underlying's levels by delta) | Native IBKR parent/child linking | The mapping drifts with time decay, IV and delta |
| C. Engine intents | The engine watches the underlying and sends a limit or market exit on the option when a bar closes beyond the level | Matches the backtest exactly; uses only verified API calls | Depends on the bot and Gateway being up |

- **Conditional orders (A):** `PriceCondition(isMore, price, conId, exch, triggerMethod)` attaches to `Order.conditions`. `Order.conditionsCancelOrder=False` submits the order when the condition is met, and `True` cancels it instead. `Order.conditionsIgnoreRth` exists **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `order.py:134-135,504-512`. Set the condition's `conId` to the **underlying** stock's, not the option's **[IBKR via extract]** [price condition](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/ref/order-conditions/price-condition), [price conditions](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/general/order-types/order-conditioning/price-conditions). IBKR's own example is exactly this case: "The condition having been satisfied, a Limit order for 1 XYZ option contract is submitted." For GTC conditional orders, "unless the order is executed on the same day as the condition trigger, the condition has to be retriggered again on the following day(s)" **[IBKR via extract]** [Conditional Orders](https://www.interactivebrokers.co.uk/en/software/tws.bak/usersguidebook/ordertypes/conditionaltest.htm).
  - **Not found:** whether conditions are accepted on bracket children (`parentId` set). Whether two conditional exits in one OCA group cancel each other on fill. Whether IBKR monitors conditions on its servers or in TWS/Gateway (if the latter, (A) is no safer than (C)).
  - The condition fires on a tick touching the price, not on a bar close. A Setup whose Stop loss is "a close beyond the level" would be tested one way and traded another **[inference]**.
- **Brackets on options (B):** IBKR's bracket page lists Options among the products in its product grid, but the extract was garbled **[IBKR via extract]** [Bracket Orders](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/general/order-types/complex-orders/bracket-orders). `bracketOrder` takes any contract **[ib_async v2.1.0]**. So brackets on `OPT` very likely work, but this was **not confirmed** first-hand. US option stops simulated by IBKR trigger by "Double bid/ask" by default: a sell stop needs two consecutive asks at or below the trigger (or one, with the ask size increasing) **[IBKR via extract]** [Trigger Methods](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/orders/trigger-methods). Option spreads are wide, so a premium stop can be hit by a quote flicker. Its fill model must use bid/ask, as for spot FX.
- **OCA groups:** `IB.oneCancelsAll(orders, ocaGroup, ocaType)` sets `ocaGroup`/`ocaType` on a list of orders **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `ib.py:753`. #29 found OCA groups link orders but isolate nothing.
- **Recommendation [inference]:** for the first options version, use (C), the engine's bar-close intents, for the Stop loss and Take profits read on the underlying, matching the backtest. Add a wide native premium Stop loss on the option as a disaster stop (for example at a set % of premium) via an ordinary bracket, so a dead bot cannot hold the option to expiry. Revisit (A) once the smoke test shows conditions on bracket children and OCA, and server-side monitoring.

## 6. Options: choosing a contract by rule

- **The chain:** `IB.reqSecDefOptParams(underlyingSymbol, futFopExchange='', underlyingSecType='STK', underlyingConId)` returns `OptionChain(exchange, underlyingConId, tradingClass, multiplier, expirations, strikes)`, one per exchange and trading class **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `ib.py:1752-1778`, `objects.py:417-423`. IBKR says this call has no throttling, unlike looping `reqContractDetails` **[IBKR via extract]** [Option chains](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/contracts/option-chains). Not every strike exists for every expiry, so qualify the chosen `Option(...)` with `qualifyContracts`, or narrow with `reqContractDetails(Option(symbol, expiry, '', right, 'SMART'))`. Whether `reqSecDefOptParams` needs a market data subscription was **not found**.
- **Expiry rules [inference]:**
  - *0DTE:* today's date is in `expirations`. Daily expirations exist for SPX, XSP, RUT, MGTN and (since 2026-05-18) DJX on Cboe, and SPY/QQQ/IWM have expirations on more weekdays than Friday. Cboe began listing Monday and Wednesday expirations for some single stocks and ETFs on 2026-01-26 **[Cboe via extract]** [DJX daily expirations](https://ir.cboe.com/news/news-details/2026/Cboe-Begins-Offering-Daily-Expirations-for-Dow-Jones-Industrial-Average-Index-Options/default.aspx), [Cboe notice 58202](https://www.cboe.com/notices/content/?id=58202). Read availability from the chain, never assume it.
  - *Nearest weekly:* the first expiry after today (or after N days).
  - *Nearest monthly:* the first expiry on the third Friday (Thursday when Friday is a holiday). For index options, monthly and weekly can be separate trading classes (SPX AM-settled vs SPXW PM-settled), so filter by `tradingClass` **[inference; SPX/SPXW from general knowledge, not checked here]**.
- **Strike rules [inference]:**
  - *ATM:* the listed strike nearest the underlying's price. *N OTM:* N listed strikes above it for calls, below for puts. Both need only the underlying's price, which comes from the stock data subscription.
  - *By delta:* needs each candidate option's delta. `reqMktData` on an option fills `Ticker.modelGreeks` (`OptionComputation`: `impliedVol`, `delta`, `gamma`, `vega`, `theta`, `undPrice`, `optPrice`) **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `ticker.py:149`, `objects.py:359-368`. This is option market data, so it needs OPRA live, or delayed data via `reqMarketDataType(3)` **[inference]**. `IB.calculateOptionPrice` and `calculateImpliedVolatility` (`ib.py:1700-1750`) let IBKR compute greeks from a given volatility. Requesting quotes for many strikes uses up market-data lines, so scan a narrow window of strikes around the money.
  - For the backtest, the 5-year history needs option prices (and IV, for delta) per strike and expiry. IBKR returns historical option data only with a live subscription **[IBKR via extract]** [FAQ 47452646](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/47452646). How far back IBKR keeps expired options' bars was **not found**. A separate options history source is likely needed (a follow-up for the map).
- **Hours:** most equity options trade 09:30–16:00 New York, and some ETF and index classes (including SPY and QQQ) to 16:15. Expiring SPX PM-settled options stop at 16:00 on expiry day **[Cboe via extract]** [Nasdaq options hours](https://nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=optionshours), [Cboe extended hours FAQ](https://www.cboe.com/document/tech-spec/document/technical-specifications/equity-options-extended-trading-hours-faq/). Equity options do not trade in pre-market or after-hours, so an option Setup's Trading window must lie inside 09:30–16:00 **[inference]**.

## 7. Options: multiplier and contract details

- **Multiplier:** standard US equity options deliver 100 shares, so a premium of 2.35 costs USD 235 per contract. Read it from `OptionChain.multiplier` or `Contract.multiplier` (a string) rather than hard-coding 100 **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `contract.py:91`, `objects.py:421`. After a split or special dividend, an option can be *adjusted*: its deliverable changes, and it moves to its own trading class (for example `XYZ1`) **[inference; not checked here]**. Skip adjusted classes by keeping only the underlying's main `tradingClass`.
- **Other fields:** `ContractDetails.minTick`, `underConId`, `realExpirationDate`, `tradingHours`, `liquidHours` and `timeZoneId` **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `contract.py:555-576`. Option tick sizes depend on price (and on the Penny Interval Program), so read `marketRuleIds` and `reqMarketRule` as for forex.
- **Sizing [inference]:** for a long option, the most a contract can lose is its premium × multiplier. With 1R = the account's risk per Trade, contracts = floor(1R ÷ (premium × multiplier)) if the whole premium is at risk, or floor(1R ÷ (expected premium loss at the Stop loss × multiplier)) using delta if the Stop loss is honoured. The second is smaller in risk but depends on the exits working.

## 8. Options: exercise, assignment, expiry and margin

- **Automatic exercise:** "Stock options expiring in the current month that are $0.01 or more in the money will be automatically exercised by the OCC" (exercise by exception, OCC Rule 805; the threshold was cut from $0.05 to $0.01 in 2008) **[IBKR via extract]** [Delivery, Exercise and Corporate Actions](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/delivery-exercise-actions.php), [SEC 34-57163](https://www.sec.gov/file/34-57163). To stop it, send a contrary instruction ("lapse") through TWS's Option Exercise window. In the API this is `IB.exerciseOptions(contract, exerciseAction=2, exerciseQuantity, account, override=1)` (1 = exercise, 2 = lapse) **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `ib.py:1780-1805`. Exercises and lapses are irrevocable, and contrary instructions are handled "on a best efforts basis". One IBKR table gives 17:25 as the broker's deadline for US options exercise requests, but cut-offs vary by exchange **[IBKR via extract]**. The OCC can remove a class from exercise by exception (a halt or corporate action), and then nothing exercises without an explicit instruction.
- **IBKR's expiry liquidations:** if the account lacks the equity to take delivery, IBKR may "1) liquidate options prior to expiration; 2) allow the options to lapse; and/or 3) allow delivery and liquidate the underlying at any time", and it decides shortly after the close on expiry day. A margin deficit can lead to liquidation of any position, not only the delivered shares **[IBKR via extract]** [Expiration & corporate action related liquidations](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/support/faq/content/expiration-corporate-action-related-liquidations-1163245142).
- **Early assignment:** US equity and ETF options are American style, so a *short* option can be assigned any day (most often the day before an ex-dividend date for short calls). A *long* option is never assigned. The bot only buys calls and puts, so early assignment does not apply to it **[inference]**. Index options such as SPX are European and cash-settled.
- **Bot rule [inference]:** never hold an option into expiry. Close every option Trade before 15:45 New York on its expiry day (earlier for 0DTE), as the "Be flat by" Closing time. Treat an exit order that has not filled by then as an incident, and send `exerciseOptions(..., exerciseAction=2, override=1)` for anything still held that is in the money. 0DTE premium decays quickly in the last hour, which the backtest must model.
- **Margin for long calls and puts:** a long listed option with nine months or less to expiry must be paid for in full and has no loan value. Options with more than nine months have a 75% loan value on equity and index options **[Cboe via extract]** [Strategy-based margin](https://www.cboe.com/en/markets/us/options/margin/strategy-based-margin/); [FINRA Notice 01-11](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/01-11) **[FINRA via extract]**. Long options can be held in a cash account, which also avoids the PDT rule **[inference]**. IBKR requires an options trading permission on the account. Which level allows long calls and puts was **not found**. Read the margin and commission from `whatIfOrder` (`ib.py:767`) before trusting any number.

- **Expected cost [inference]:** USD 14.50 a month for streaming consolidated stocks plus OPRA, falling to USD 4.50 in any month with at least USD 30 in commissions. The non-professional snapshot bundle also covers CME/CBOT/COMEX/NYMEX top-of-book **snapshots**, which is not streaming.
- **Free real-time is not the NBBO:** the Cboe One + IEX feed shows only those venues' quotes and trades, so its volume and highs/lows are a fraction of the consolidated tape's. Volume Rule blocks on stocks should use consolidated data, or accept the difference between backtest and live **[inference]**.
- **Delayed data in the API:** `reqMarketDataType(3)` (delayed) or `4` (delayed frozen) **[ib_async v2.1.0]** `ib.py:1324-1338`. US delays are 15 minutes. Delayed data works with `reqMktData` and `reqHistoricalData` but not with tick-by-tick data **[IBKR via extract]** [Delayed data](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/md/delayed-data). Delayed is fine for building and smoke-testing, not for trading 1-minute Setups.
- **The Paper account and the second username (#42):** market data is attached to each username. So the stock/OPRA subscriptions follow the same route as MNQ's CME data: subscribe once on the live username, and share it with the Paper account. A second *live* username would pay for its own subscriptions **[inference]**.

## What this means for the map

- **Stocks are the smaller change [inference].** They reuse #42's Bracket orders, Take profits and sizing (whole shares instead of contracts). They need:
  1. a stock Trading day and an NYSE holiday/early-close calendar;
  2. an outside-RTH flag and a stop-limit Stop loss for any Setup active outside 09:30–16:00;
  3. a shortable check, the Rule 201 restriction and a borrow-fee line for overnight shorts;
  4. a day-trade counter or stand-down while the PDT transition lasts, or a Paper account kept at USD 25,000 or more;
  5. consolidated data (USD 14.50/month) if Rule blocks read volume.
- **Options are a larger change [inference].** They add:
  1. a contract chooser (expiry rule plus strike rule) run at Signal time on the chain;
  2. exits computed on the underlying and carried out on the option by engine intents, with a premium disaster stop;
  3. premium × multiplier sizing;
  4. a forced close on expiry day and a lapse instruction as a fallback;
  5. OPRA data and an options history source for the backtest;
  6. a fill model on option bid/ask.
