What the rules saw, step by step

Levels

The rules as this prototype implements them, and where it had to interpret them
  • Bars: 5m, 15m and daily bars are built from the 1m bars. 5m and 15m are clock-aligned. A daily bar is a CME trading day, 18:00–17:00 New York.
  • Bias: picked by the "Bias rule" setting. No Bias means no Signals.
    • Swing + leg origin (default): a daily close beyond the most recent confirmed daily Swing point (2 bars each side), or a daily close beyond the low that started the leg up to the latest swing high (the lowest low of the 2 days before it) flips it bearish, and the mirror flips it bullish. Added because the swing rule alone found nothing in a steady fall: no daily swing low forms, so there is nothing to break.
    • Swing (the original rule): only a close beyond the most recent confirmed daily Swing point. Can stay empty, or stale, for weeks after a top or bottom.
    • Previous-day break: yesterday closed above the day before's high (bullish) or below its low (bearish); otherwise the last Bias stands. Fastest, but flips on one-day bounces.
    The first days of a file have no Bias until enough daily bars exist, so fetch a few weeks before the first day you review.
  • Levels: previous trading day high/low, Asia high/low (20:00–00:00), London high/low (02:00–05:00). The chart marks these two sessions, plus the NY cash session (09:30–16:00), with a coloured strip along the top and dashed lines at each start and end. Use "Whole trading day" to see all three.
  • Sweep: a 5m bar opening from 09:30 whose wick is ≥1 tick through a level on the side opposite the Bias (lows for a long). Interpretation: a level that was already taken before the sweep still counts, as written. The trace flags it, and the "sweep only untaken levels" setting switches that off. A later sweep that doesn't go beyond the current extreme is ignored.
  • MSS: a later 5m bar closing beyond the most recent confirmed 5m Swing point on the other side, found before the sweep's extreme and since 18:00. A 5m bar beyond the extreme moves the extreme there and re-measures the swing. Interpretation: the sweep bar itself can't be the MSS bar.
  • Void: after the MSS, any 1m bar beyond the extreme voids the chain. This applies while waiting for the FVG, while the entry rests, and after a stop-out. A new sweep is then needed.
  • FVG: 1m, gap ≥ 4 ticks, middle candle closing in the gap's direction, range ≥ 1.5× the average of the 20 bars before it, body ≥ 60%. The third candle must be in the Window. The last 15m bar closed before the third candle must not close against the Trade. Interpretation of "at or after the MSS bar": the middle candle opens at or after the 5m MSS bar opens, and the third candle closes at or after it closes.
  • Draw: the nearest untaken level on the Trade's side at least the Minimum framework from the entry. None means no Signal; later FVGs in the Window are still checked.
  • Entry, stop, targets: limit at the near edge, stop 2 ticks beyond the sweep extreme, reward-to-risk ≥ 1.0. TP1 halfway, rounded toward the entry. TP2 1 tick before the draw. 1 contract means a single leg at TP2. Contracts = floor(2% × $5,000 ÷ risk per contract), max 5. Break-even after TP1.
  • Entry model (setting; the user chose FVG only as the default): any FVG that passes the FVG checks, in the Bias direction, with its third candle in a Window and a Draw on liquidity. There is no sweep or MSS: the stop is 2 ticks beyond the FVG's first candle, and a resting entry is cancelled if price reaches that stop level first. Sweep → MSS → FVG is the first agreed chain; with it, the stop is beyond the sweep extreme.
  • G · EMA 200 + MACD (comparison row; "Show EMA 200 + MACD" draws the EMA 200, a MACD pane and its Trades on the chart, in teal, next to the Silver Bullet): the DAFT Trade documentation's strategy on 15m bars built from the 1m bars. Long: close above a rising EMA 200 (higher than 10 bars ago), ADX(14) > 20, MACD histogram negative for ≥ 3 bars, then the MACD crosses above its signal with both lines below zero; market entry at the next 1m open. Stop at the pullback low − 0.25 × ATR(14), skipped if wider than 3 × ATR. Half at 1.5R and stop to entry, the rest at 3R or when the MACD crosses back; everything closed on a 15m close beyond the EMA 200. Mirror for shorts. Interpretations: entries only 09:45–12:00 and 13:30–15:50 NY, flattened at 15:50; the map's risk settings (2% of $5,000, max 5 MNQ) instead of the docs' 0.5–1%; no news rule. The EMA 200 needs 200 bars of 15m history (about 3 days) before the first Signal.
  • Line ("Candles: Line" in the Indicators panel; display only): the closes of the displayed bars joined by a line, on the normal time axis, so sessions, overlays, panes and all the Silver Bullet markings stay. With "Line from Heikin Ashi closes" it joins the Heikin Ashi closes instead (brown line), which smooths it; every rule still uses the real bars.
  • Area ("Candles: Area"; display only): the line chart with the space under the closes shaded, on the normal time axis. "Area from Heikin Ashi closes" uses the Heikin Ashi closes instead (brown).
  • Renko ("Candles: Renko" in the Indicators panel; display only): bricks of a set size (default 5 points) built from the 1m closes of the selected trading day. A new brick when a close is a full brick beyond the last one in the trend's direction; a reversal when a close is a full brick beyond the last brick's other end. With "Renko from Heikin Ashi closes" the bricks are built from the 1m bars' Heikin Ashi closes ((O + H + L + C) ÷ 4, smoothed) instead of their real closes, which filters out small back-and-forth moves; the Signal's prices stay real, so its lines may sit off the bricks. Bricks have no time axis, so the chart shows only the bricks completed in the current view, with the Liquidity levels, the Signal's lines, the fills and exits on the brick they fell in, and the Window's bricks shaded; sessions, indicator overlays and panes are hidden. The labels under the bricks are the times they completed.
  • Kagi ("Candles: Kagi" in the Indicators panel; display only): one line from the selected trading day's 1m closes that extends while closes make new extremes and turns when a close reverses by the reversal amount (default 10 points). It turns thick (yang, green) where a rise passes the previous shoulder and thin (yin, red) where a fall passes the previous waist. With "Kagi from Heikin Ashi closes" the line follows the 1m bars' Heikin Ashi closes instead of their real closes, which filters out small back-and-forth moves; the Signal's prices stay real. Like Renko and Point & Figure it has no time axis, so it shows the Liquidity levels, the Signal's lines, fills and exits on the segment they fell in, and the Window's segments shaded, and hides sessions, overlays and panes. The labels under the line are the times each segment started.
  • Point & Figure ("Candles: Point & Figure" in the Indicators panel; display only): columns of X (rising) and O (falling) from the selected trading day's 1m closes, on a grid of box size points (default 5). A column extends while closes reach one more box; a new column in the other direction starts when a close reverses by the reversal count (default 3 boxes), one box past the last column's extreme. With "P&F from Heikin Ashi closes" the columns are built from the 1m bars' Heikin Ashi closes instead of their real closes, which filters out small back-and-forth moves; the Signal's prices stay real. Like Renko it has no time axis, so it shows the Liquidity levels, the Signal's lines, fills and exits on the column they fell in, and the Window's columns shaded, and hides sessions, overlays and panes. The labels under the columns are the times they started.
  • Heikin Ashi candles (display only; "Candles" in the Indicators panel): close = (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4, open = the midpoint of the previous Heikin Ashi candle's open and close, high and low = the extremes of the real high/low and those two. They smooth the trend but are not traded prices: every rule, level, FVG, entry, stop and indicator still uses the real bars, so lines may not touch the drawn candles. Hovering a bar shows both.
  • Volume pane (display only, as in the DAFT Trade documentation): contracts per displayed bar, green for up candles and red for down, darker when at least 1.5× the 20-bar average, which is drawn as a grey line. 5m and 15m bars carry the sum of their 1m bars' volume. Needs the volume column.
  • Stochastic and OBV panes (display only; not in the DAFT Trade documentation's indicator pages, so standard definitions): slow Stochastic 14,3,3 of the displayed bars, %K blue and %D orange, with 80/20 lines. OBV adds a bar's volume when it closes above the previous close and subtracts it when below; it is cumulated from the start of the file, so read its slope, not its level. OBV needs the volume column.
  • ADX and ATR panes (display only): Wilder's ADX 14 with +DI (green) and −DI (red) of the displayed bars, lines at 20 and 25 and the area under 20 (no trend, per the DAFT Trade documentation) shaded; Wilder's ATR 14 in points, with its value per MNQ ($2 a point). Both match the ADX and ATR the EMA·MACD strategy uses on 15m.
  • Keltner and Donchian Channels (display only; not in the DAFT Trade documentation, so standard settings): Keltner 20, 2 is the EMA 20 of the close (dashed) ± 2 × ATR 10 (Wilder), in purple. Donchian 20 is the highest high and lowest low of the last 20 bars, this bar included, with their middle dashed, drawn as steps, in orange.
  • Parabolic SAR and Supertrend (display only; not in the DAFT Trade documentation, so standard settings): Wilder's Parabolic SAR with acceleration 0.02, +0.02 per new extreme, max 0.2, drawn as dots (green below price in an uptrend, red above in a downtrend). Supertrend 10, 3: (high + low) ÷ 2 ± 3 × ATR(10), the band only tightening while price stays on its side, green under price in an uptrend and red above in a downtrend, flipping when a close crosses it.
  • Ichimoku (display only; not in the DAFT Trade documentation, so the standard 9, 26, 52): Tenkan (mid of the 9-bar high-low range, blue), Kijun (26-bar, dark red), the cloud between Senkou A ((Tenkan + Kijun) ÷ 2) and Senkou B (mid of the 52-bar range), both shifted 26 bars forward, green when A is above B, and Chikou (the close shifted 26 bars back, dotted). Chikou shows the close 26 bars later, so it is hindsight, as on any Ichimoku chart.
  • Bollinger Bands (display only, from the DAFT Trade documentation): SMA 20 of the displayed bars' closes (dashed) ± 2 standard deviations, shaded between the bands.
  • VWAP and RSI (display only, no rule uses them; settings from the DAFT Trade documentation): VWAP = Σ(typical price × volume) ÷ Σ volume on the 1m bars, reset at 18:00 NY (the start of the Trading day) or at 09:30 NY (then shown 09:30–17:00 only); a 5m or 15m bar shows the VWAP at its last minute. It needs the file's volume column. RSI 14 (Wilder) of the displayed bars in its own pane, with 70/30 lines.
  • MACD pane (display only, no rule uses it): MACD 12,26,9 under the chart. "Chart timeframe" uses the displayed 1m, 5m or 15m bars. "15m" is the EMA·MACD strategy's MACD; on 1m and 5m charts each bar shows the last 15m bar closed before it. Turning on "Show EMA 200 + MACD" switches the pane to 15m if it was off.
  • Levers for more entries (settings, and the Strategy presets A–F, compared side by side in "Compare strategies". Each preset builds on the one before, except F, which adds the fallback to D):
    • PM Window: a second Window, 14:00–15:00 NY, with the same rules. An unfilled entry is cancelled at the end of the Window it came from.
    • When no Bias: trade both sides (FVG model only): FVGs in either direction, each still needing its own Draw on liquidity.
    • Looser FVG: displacement 1.0× instead of 1.5×, gap 2 ticks instead of 4, and no 15m check.
    • Fallback (FVG model only): if a Window has produced no Signal 15 minutes before it ends, take the FVG of that Window with the strongest displacement that fails one check only, if price hasn't yet traded through its entry. The Trade is flagged "fallback" with the check it failed.
  • Simplifications: fills are checked on 1m bars, not 1-second bars. A limit fills only when price trades 1 tick through it. A bar touching both the stop and a target counts as a stop ("ambiguous bar"). Stops get 1 tick of slippage. No costs, no daily compounding, no Stand-down, no news days. Unfilled entries are cancelled at 11:00 and open Trades are flattened at 15:50.
